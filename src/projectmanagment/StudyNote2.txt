--- Project Management Tools & Roles ---

1. Gantt Charts:
These are bar charts used to illustrate project schedules. They show start and end dates, task durations, and dependencies.

2. PERT Charts:
PERT (Program Evaluation Review Technique) charts help visualize task sequences and identify dependencies between tasks.

3. Risk Management:
Risk management involves identifying potential issues and preparing mitigation strategies. A Risk Register documents risks, impacts, and action plans.

4. Resource Planning:
A resource plan defines all the people, tools, and materials required to complete the project. Planning helps prevent resource shortages.

5. Roles in a Project:
   - Sponsor: Provides funding and high-level approval.
   - Stakeholder: Anyone impacted by the project.
   - Project Manager: Oversees the team and ensures the project stays on track.
   - Team Lead: Coordinates tasks and supports the team.

6. Importance of Updates:
Documents like the Risk Register and Project Schedule should be reviewed and updated regularly to reflect the project's actual status.
